Good to know

Additional subscription fees may apply

Your package covers my design and setup work. The platforms and apps your business runs on charge their own fees, which you pay directly to them. These are separate from your package price.

Website platform plan

For example, a paid Shopify plan is required before your store can go live and take orders. Billed monthly by the platform.

Apps & tools

Apps your business needs to operate, like DSers for dropshipping, product review apps, QR code tools, email marketing or booking apps. Many charge a monthly fee.

Domain name

Your yourbusiness.com address is renewed yearly with the company you buy it from.

Payment processing fees

Stripe, Square, PayPal and Shopify Payments take a small percentage of each sale.

Anything you want added outside your package is an extra cost. I'll let you know about any app fees before installing it, and nothing gets added without your OK. All accounts and subscriptions stay in your name.
