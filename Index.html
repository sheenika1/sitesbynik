Questions

How does it work after I book?+

Pay your 50% deposit and you'll get a short form for your logo, photos, text, prices and links. I build your site and send you a preview link, you send your revisions, and once you pay the remaining 50%, your site goes live.

How long will my site take?+

At least 2 weeks, including revisions. Sending your info and changes on time keeps your launch on schedule.

Do you set up payments?+

Yes. You open a Stripe, Square or PayPal account in your name so the money goes straight to you, and I connect your payment buttons or checkout. I run a test purchase before launch so you know it works.

Are there other fees besides my package?+

Yes. Your package covers my design and setup. Platforms and apps charge their own fees, paid by you directly to them: a paid Shopify plan is required before a store can go live, apps like DSers for dropshipping, review apps, QR code tools, or email and booking apps may charge monthly, your domain renews yearly, and payment processors take a small fee per sale. I'll tell you about any app cost before adding it, and everything stays in your name.

How do revisions work?+

Send all your changes together in one list. Each list is one round. Text, photos, colors, fonts, sections and buttons are included. New pages, a full redesign or changes after you approve the final site cost extra. Extra rounds are $50, and please send revisions within 5 days to keep your launch on track.

What do I need to send you?+

Your logo and brand colors, photos of you, your products or your work, a little about your business, services and prices, your social links, and your domain if you have one. Don't have something yet? Let me know and we'll figure it out.

Do you write the words for my site?+

You send me the basics, and I'll clean them up and shape them into sections that read well.

Can I upgrade later?+

Absolutely. Start with Starter or Launch, and when you're ready to grow, you only pay the difference to upgrade.

How do I pay?+

50% deposit to start and 50% before launch, by card through Stripe.

What's your refund policy?+

All payments are non-refundable, including deposits, add-ons and retainer months already billed. If your plans change or you need to cancel, what you've paid can be used as credit toward a different website or another Sites by Nik service within 6 months of your payment. Monthly retainers can be cancelled anytime with 30 days' notice to stop future charges.
